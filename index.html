About Me

I'm Chris Stutz, a protein engineer with 10 years of industry experience and a PhD focused on antibody discovery via display technologies. I publish under my legal name, Charles Christopher Stutz.

I lead protein optimization and discovery campaigns from strategy through execution, working with cross-functional teams to coordinate timelines and product profiles to make sure our work fits into the bigger picture. My technical toolkit includes phage, yeast, and mRNA display across antibodies, TCRs, and macrocyclic peptides.

I manage technical teams and focus on making sure optimization projects land on time and serve the broader program goals

Research Interests

My research interests primarily lie in fine tuning libraries and selection strategies to fit the needs of the pogram.
